Key Incidents — Northern Virginia

2022 — Herndon, Fairfax County
Jasper Aaron Lynch
26-year-old White man shot during a mental health crisis. One of two Fairfax County mental health–related fatal police shootings identified in the Washington Post database (2015–2024). No co-responder clinician was present. No charges filed.
2023 — Henrico County
Irvo Otieno
28-year-old Black man with mental illness killed while being admitted to Central State Hospital. Ten people charged — zero convictions. All charges were nolle prossed or resulted in acquittal by November 2024. Led to significant national debate about systemic accountability.
2024 — Reston, Fairfax County
Sydney Wilson
33-year-old Black woman shot during a mental health crisis welfare check. CIT-trained officer responded alone — no co-responder clinician present. Led to police auditor's landmark recommendation to reconsider police role in behavioral health calls.
2024 — Rosslyn, Arlington
Cedric Butler
27-year-old shot at Rosslyn Metro Station entrance after displaying a knife. Regional Critical Incident Response Team investigated.

Section 6

Policy Recommendations & Advocacy Actions

Evidence-based recommendations drawn from program evaluations, state reports, and disability advocacy research. Prioritized for the Northern Virginia and Virginia policy context.

01
Fund 24/7 Co-Responder Coverage
Expand co-responder teams in Arlington and Fairfax to 24/7 operations. Current MOST program (Arlington) operates 1pm–9pm weekdays only — both the Lynch (2022) and Wilson (2024) fatal encounters fell outside covered hours. Model after CAHOOTS 24/7 model. Source: ARLnow 2024.
02
Convene the Marcus Alert Evaluation Task Force
Virginia law required creation of a Marcus Alert Evaluation Task Force — it has never met. Disability advocacy organizations (Arc of Northern Virginia, ECNV) should demand immediate convening and public reporting on outcomes data, including disaggregated disability and race data. Source: BHC Marcus Alert Report.
03
Mandate Disability Training in All Police Agencies
Only 20 states require law enforcement training on IDD/autism. Virginia should mandate specific training on intellectual disability, autism, and communication differences for all officers — not just CIT-designated personnel. Source: The Arc, 2025.
04
Increase CSB Funding Equity
Replace the flat $600K/CSB formula with population-adjusted and need-based funding. Current formula is explicitly identified as inadequate in the BHC September 2025 report. 13 CSBs have received zero planning funds — jeopardizing the 2028 statewide implementation deadline.
05
Implement Federal Disability Tracking
Advocate for federal legislation requiring mandatory tracking of disability status in all use-of-force and police killing reports. FBI's National Use-of-Force Data Collection (2019) does not include disability. Without data, systemic change is unmeasurable. Source: Treatment Advocacy Center.
06
Scale 988 / Crisis Line Access
Virginia's 911→988 transfer rate is only ~6%, far below targets. Advocate for dispatcher training, system integration, and public awareness campaigns. 988 call volume in Virginia grew 148% Sept 2023→Sept 2024 — but chat (39%) and text (37%) answer rates lag severely. Source: DBHDS FY2026 Report.
⚠ Key Data Limitations — Interpret With Care

This dashboard presents the best available public data on police encounters involving people with mental illness and disabilities. Every dataset below has significant structural limitations that affect interpretation. Do not treat any figure as definitive.

  • WaPo Fatal Force covers only fatal shootings — excludes Taser, restraint, vehicle deaths
  • MI flag in WaPo data relies on media reports; academic studies find rates 1.5–2× higher
  • No federal mandatory tracking of disability status in police killings exists
  • WaPo public CSV ends December 2024; 2025–2026 data from MPV only
  • Black victims are less likely to be recorded as having mental illness, compounding disparities
  • Disability % (33–50%) is from academic/advocacy case reviews, not continuous surveillance
